# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of local, booking-based health and fitness businesses in Canada and the US: physiotherapists, chiropractors, massage therapists, and studio and gym owners. They are good at their work, busy with clients, and have no time to manage their Google profile, listings, reviews or website. They are evaluating whether to hand that work to someone they can trust.

## Product Purpose

Get Found is a monthly service by Altura Marketing LTD (BC, Canada) that gets these businesses showing up on Google Maps and in AI tools like ChatGPT when people nearby search for what they do. Success for the client is more calls, direction requests and bookings, with under 10 minutes a month of their own time. Success for the website is a booked free visibility check or a scorecard request.

## Positioning

- **Transparency:** a plain-language monthly email and one-page report showing what was done and what changed; the client owns their profile, listings, domain and content; no ranking promises; month-to-month available.
- **Health and fitness specialism:** follows the advertising rules of the client's professional college or licensing board, and never sees or stores client or health information.
- **AI visibility:** tracks, every month, whether the business appears in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Google AI Mode answers, alongside Google Maps.
- **Price and effort:** a fraction of typical local SEO retainers, with almost all the work done for the owner.

## Operating Context

- Entry point: the free visibility check, a one-page scorecard emailed within one business day: where the business shows for five local searches, how it compares with three nearby competitors, and whether ChatGPT mentions it. An optional 15-minute call (booking link) is offered for owners with questions; no call is required.
- Monthly cycle: Google profile kept complete and active, automatic review requests after visits, listings corrected across Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp, Facebook and directories, one blog post answering questions people in the client's area are asking about services like theirs (researched from local search questions; the client may reuse it on social media, email or ads), and a 3-line email plus one-page report.
- Optional website build: a new site that then runs on Get Found with hosting and updates included, quoted on the call.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prices are shown in CAD and USD and are provisional: they depend on the pilot clients. Canadian clients pay GST.
- The booking link, contact email and scorecard form backend are not set up yet; the site must not launch without them.
- Marketing emails require explicit opt-in (Canada's anti-spam law, CASL); scorecard requests alone do not grant it.
- Privacy and terms pages are drafts awaiting review.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Get Found, a service by Altura Marketing LTD. Domain: getfoundwithaltura.com.
- Voice: plain, direct, honest; no hype and no ranking guarantees.
- The founder writes about marketing in health and fitness on Substack.

## Evidence on Hand

- Pilot clients are running now. No testimonials, client names, logos or results may be shown until real, approved ones exist (expected within weeks). The site's proof section stays hidden until then.
- The sample monthly report uses illustrative numbers and must stay labelled as such.
- The price anchor ("local SEO retainers average around US$1,500 a month", Ahrefs) must be checked against its source before launch.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work: every claim is something the owner can check in their own report or accounts.
2. The owner's time is the scarcest resource; ask for as little of it as possible.
3. Respect the profession: comply with professional advertising rules and keep health information out entirely.
4. Never fabricate proof, results or urgency; honesty is the competitive edge.
5. Lead with outcomes owners pay for (calls, directions, bookings), not vanity metrics.
